## style

Bright flat casual 2D with soft gradients and gentle bevels: clean geometric blocks with a subtle top highlight and soft shadow, rounded UI panels with layered frames, and crisp sans-serif type. No pixel art, no outlines heavier than 3 px.

## palette

Sky gradient #8FD8F8 to #3F7FD9; blocks coral #FF7A59, sun #FFC94A, mint #3DDC97, lilac #9B8CFF; UI panel navy #1E2A4A with frame #F4E7C5; text #FFFFFF; accent gold #FFB627; danger #FF4D5E.

## mood

Calm, satisfying and upbeat: a relaxing rhythm that turns tense as the tower narrows.
