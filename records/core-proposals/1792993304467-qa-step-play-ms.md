status: proposed

## target

QACore (`ogw step` and the telemetry `play_at` of `qa-core`)

## kind

bug

## problem

In step mode, `ogw step <instance> 50` reports `advanced 50 ms` per step, but the `play ms` it prints (the `play ms` of `ogw telemetry --summary`) rises by about 75 per step. Seen on Block Stack (core 0.4.0, macOS client, 1440x900 test screen): 12 steps of 50 ms took `play ms` from 132538 to 133363 (+75 per step), while the content tower, which advances with the sum of `TimeCore.get_game_frame_ms`, moved 35 units per step at 700 units/s, which is exactly 50 ms. `qa-core` says `play_at` and the durations of `goal_intervals` and `idle_spans` are measured in advanced ms, so a step of 50 ms should add 50. The play agent read the gap as the game running faster than the step (loop create-1792989369596 defect `step-advances-more-game-time`), and the telemetry metrics measured in step-mode play (`reward_interval_ms`, `goal_interval_ms`, `restart_delay_ms`) are about 1.5 times the game time they span

## proposal

`qa-core` `### telemetry`: in step mode, the advanced ms that `play_at`, `latency_ms`, `idle_spans` and `goal_intervals` count is the sum of the ms that `step` advanced; a test case: after `step`(50) twelve times, the `play_at` difference of two marks put before the first and after the last step is 600

## workaround

None in content code. `play-guide.md` `## play mode` states that the tower moves by the step ms of game time per step while the printed `play ms` rises by about 75 per 50 ms step
